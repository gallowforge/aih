# aih: the identity

**The one idea.** A memory palace run like a library. Every AI chat you have had is filed in a card catalogue, and the librarian finds any card in milliseconds.

**Motif.** The card catalogue and its furniture. Index cards with the rod hole at the bottom, kraft guide cards with staggered tabs, the oak drawer with its brass label holder, cup pull and rod knob, a violet rubber stamp. Every feature is one part of the library: search is a drawer section, the numbers are the cabinet face, stats is the circulation ledger, a Markdown file is a card opened into a filed note, collect is the returns trolley's hourly round, check is a stamped withdrawal slip, and the architecture is the building's floor plan.

**Signature visual.** A catalogue drawer pulled open. The query is typed on the drawer's label (`fts5 tokenizer`), the best match stands up front typed in full, and the runners-up rise behind it showing their top lines. A violet stamp carries one measured fact: 3.2 ms, the median search over 100,000 messages.

**Type.** Two families, both chosen for the library:
- **Libre Caslon** (Display for headlines, Text for small serif and small caps). Caslon is the classic English book face, the voice of the library's printed signs and ledgers.
- **Courier Prime** for everything typed. Catalogue cards were typed, and Courier began as a typewriter face; Prime is the refined cut with a real bold and italic.

Hierarchy comes from size jumps of 1:3 or more, never from extra families.

**Palette** (60 / 30 / 10):

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Field, 60 | warm card stock | `#ECE3CF` (card stock `#FBF7EC`) |
| Support, 30 | typewriter ink | `#241D17` (faded `#6B6053`, pencil `#9A8E7E`) |
| Support, 30 | oak and brass, for the cabinet only | `#9C683B`, `#B48C45` |
| Accent, 10 | violet stamp ink | `#5A3AA8` |

Violet is spent only on what aih found or proved: matching words in a result, a stamp with a measured number, the SAFE verdict, the route a search takes through the stacks, and the ledger's ribbon.

**Voice.** A quietly clever librarian with a dry wit. Pencil notes in the margins ("best match up front", "away from home, so it tries the next name", "then ask the librarian"), never jokes at the cost of a fact. Every number on a card, label or slip comes from a real capture.

**Rules.** Light, paper-first, no dark cards. Objects lie on the paper with real light from the top left and soft contact shadows, rotated a degree at most. Text stays left-aligned on a visible grid. Each image keeps one detail for a careful viewer: the Dewey call number 006.3 (artificial intelligence) on the drawer label, the drawer left ajar on the 3.2 ms figure, the double-underlined ledger totals, the title block that gives the building's scale as one Python file.
